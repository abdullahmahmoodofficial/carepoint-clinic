Our Story

Started With One Room, Built on One Principle

In 2008, Dr. Imran Sheikh opened a single-room clinic with a simple rule: treat every patient like your own family. No inflated bills, no scary jargon, no tests you don't need.

Eighteen years later, that room has grown into a full family clinic — but the rule hasn't changed. Today, three physicians care for over 85,000 registered patients, from newborns getting their first vaccination to grandparents managing diabetes with dignity.
